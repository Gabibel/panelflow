What PanelFlow is

PanelFlow is a personal library and a reader for the online reading sites (manga, webtoons, light novels) and viewing sites (anime) that you choose to visit yourself. It comes in three forms sharing the same account: a browser extension, this website, and a mobile app.

PanelFlow does not host, store or distribute any work. It indexes no catalogue. What you read is loaded from the site you opened, in your browser, exactly as without PanelFlow; the service only keeps the addresses of the pages you added to your library and your position in them. On the website, the cover images shown on your shelf are relayed on the fly from the original site or from AniList's public catalogue, and kept for twenty-four hours at most in the server's temporary memory, never on disk; the extension and the app load them directly from their source.

The service is free. It sells nothing, shows no advertising and contains no purchases.
