Help

A question, a site that does not open in the reader, something to correct or to erase: write to 1animoment@gmail.com, the address of the person who publishes PanelFlow.

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, your library stays on the device and nothing is sent to our servers. An account is for finding the same library on the iPhone, in the Chrome extension and on this website, and for connecting AniList or MyAnimeList. You need to be 15 or older to create one.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgotten your password?" and enter your address. The link in the e-mail works once, for 60 minutes. Changing the password signs out every device: each one will ask for the new password.

Getting my data

In the app, "Settings", then "Account & sync", then "Export": a complete backup (series, progress, history, settings) that PanelFlow can restore. On this website, the "Export" button above the library gives the same backup, and also a spreadsheet other software can read.

Deleting my account

In the app, "Settings", then "Account & sync", then "Delete my account". On this website, "Settings", in the "Account" section. You are asked for your password again, then everything attached to the account is erased immediately and for good: library, progress, history, preferences and AniList or MyAnimeList connections. The privacy policy says what is kept, and for how long.

AniList and MyAnimeList

In the settings, "Trackers" ("Connected accounts" on this website), with a PanelFlow account. The token the service gives us is encrypted in our database, and "Disconnect" deletes it from our servers. You can also revoke the access from your AniList or MyAnimeList account.

Offline chapters

The reader's save button keeps a chapter for reading with no network. It stays on the device, is never copied anywhere else, and is erased on its own after 90 days. In the app, the "Offline" tab lists them and lets you remove them sooner.

A site does not open in the reader

"Settings", then "Report a problem" in the app, or "Report an issue" in the extension's menu. The report opens in your mail app: you read it before sending it, and nothing leaves without you.

Does PanelFlow host any works?

No. PanelFlow hosts, lists and recommends no works: it only changes how the pages you open yourself are displayed, and keeps your place in them. To report content, see the legal notice.

How your data is protected

  • Your password is never stored: only a fingerprint of it (bcrypt) is.
  • Every connection goes over HTTPS, and the site refuses to be loaded in clear.
  • AniList and MyAnimeList tokens are encrypted in the database.
  • No advertising, no audience measurement, no cookies.
  • On the iPhone, your session is kept in the iOS Keychain.
