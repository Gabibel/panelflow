Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This is a translation of the French politique de confidentialité, which prevails if the two ever differ.

This page describes what PanelFlow records about you, why, for how long, who can access it and how to delete it. It is written from what the software actually does: every item below is a table in the database or a key in your device's storage, and nothing else is recorded.

In two sentences: PanelFlow records your e-mail address, your library and your reading progress, because that is the service. It uses no cookies, no audience measurement and no advertising, sells nothing, and passes your data to no one but the technical providers named below.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the publisher of PanelFlow, identified in the legal notice. For any question or request about your data: contact address to be provided.

3. What stays on your device

Part of the same data is kept locally so the service works offline and responds without waiting for the server:

  • Website: in your browser's localStorage, with your session token (what keeps you signed in), and the theme and language you chose.
  • Extension: in the extension's storage, with a copy of your library, progress and preferences, and the session token; and, if you save chapters to read offline, their images, erased automatically 90 days after they were saved.
  • Mobile app: in the app's storage, with the same copy and the cover images shown.
  • Extension and app: a short technical record of recent events (errors, syncs), which only leaves the device if you send it yourself from "Report a problem".

This data leaves the device only towards PanelFlow's server, for syncing. Signing out erases it once the last sync has gone through; if changes could not be sent (server unreachable), they stay on the device and are sent the next time you sign in with that account, which the device tells you when you sign out. Uninstalling the extension or the app erases everything.

4. Cookies and trackers

PanelFlow uses no cookies. The session is carried by a token kept in your browser's local storage (see above), which is sent to PanelFlow's server and to it alone. This storage is strictly necessary for the service you asked for (it is what keeps you signed in) and is therefore exempt from consent under article 82 of the French Data Protection Act. That is why no banner is shown to you: there is nothing to accept or refuse.

There is no audience measurement, no pixel, no advertising, no social sharing button, no font or script loaded from a third party. The website, the extension and the app load only their own files; the only thing displayed from elsewhere is the cover images of your series (section 7).

The reading sites you visit have their own cookies and trackers, over which PanelFlow has no control, except to block some of them (section 7).

6. AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu

Nothing is exchanged with these services until you click "Connect". If you do, you are sent to their authorisation page; they then give PanelFlow a token limited to your list. PanelFlow uses it to:

  • read your list, to find your series and pre-fill the details of a series you add;
  • write your progress to it (chapter reached, status, score) for the series you linked, and only those; a series not recognised with certainty is never written without you confirming the match;
  • look up a cover image in their public catalogue, for series whose site provides none.

What these services do with your list is governed by their own policies (AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu). "Disconnect" deletes the token from our servers; you can also revoke the authorisation from the settings of the service concerned.

7. The sites you visit

PanelFlow works inside the pages of the reading sites you open, to offer its reader there. To do so, the extension and the app read the content of the page shown (the chapter list, the chapter's images), on your device and without sending it to PanelFlow. What is sent to the server is what you ask to save: the series added, the chapter reached.

Two exceptions, each triggered by you: when you add a series, the server may read the series page once to extract its title and cover; and every day, it visits the page of each of your series to look for a new chapter. In both cases it is the server that visits, with its own address, not yours.

Cover images. On the website and in the app, they are relayed by PanelFlow's server, which keeps them in memory for twenty-four hours at most and never on disk; the original site then sees the server's address, not yours. In the extension, they are loaded directly from the original site, which then sees your IP address, as during a visit.

Search. Your words are sent exactly as you typed them, with nothing added. From the app, they go straight from your phone to DuckDuckGo; elsewhere, or if that fails, PanelFlow's server passes them to Brave Search or DuckDuckGo, with its own address. When a search asks for its results to be checked, the server also visits the first five to say whether PanelFlow can read them. Searches are not recorded.

Ad blocking. PanelFlow blocks some advertising and redirects. In the extension, only on the reading sites it supports and those you add to it, and nowhere else; in the app, in the pages opened by its built-in browser. This blocking relies on a list of advertising domains applied by your browser or your phone and on rules that run in the page; it does not watch your browsing and reports nothing.

8. Retention

The periods are those in the table in section 2. The general rule: data tied to the account lives as long as the account and disappears with it. A few exceptions have their own clock: a removed series (30 days, then erased with its progress and history), reset and address-change requests (valid for 60 minutes, erased 7 days later), the anti-abuse counters (72 hours) and the security log (30 days at most).

An account is never deleted automatically for inactivity. Database backups, if the provider makes them, are kept according to its own periods and used only to restore the service after an incident.

9. Your rights

You have the rights provided by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), and you can exercise most of them without writing to us:

Access and portability
Library → Export: a complete backup in JSON format, which can be imported again (library, progress, history, shelves), with an "account" section holding the rest: e-mail address, creation date, preferences, connected services, chapters found, notification subscriptions and removed series. Access tokens and keys are not copied into the file: that would put a key to your account in it. There is also a MyAnimeList export (XML) and a spreadsheet (CSV).
Rectification
Each series can be edited from the library; progress, from the reader or the series details; the password and the e-mail address, from the settings (the address changes once the link received at the new address has been opened).
Erasure
Settings → Account → "Delete my account". Deletion is immediate and final: it erases the account and everything attached to it. All that remains, with no link to the account, are the anti-abuse counters (72 hours at most, as a hash) and the lines of the security log (30 days at most, with no e-mail address). "Clear history" only deletes the reading history.
Objection and restriction
The optional processing, notifications and trackers, is turned off in one click. For the rest, write to us.

For all of the above, and for any request that has no place in the app, write to contact address to be provided. We answer within a month. If you consider that your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or with the authority of the country where you live.

10. Security

  • All communications are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as a bcrypt hash; no one, including the publisher, can read them.
  • Sign-in, sign-up and reset attempts are limited per address and per account; the log that records them contains no e-mail address and no full IP address.
  • The server only fetches public pages and refuses any address pointing to a private network.
  • The session token never leaves your device except towards PanelFlow's server; the pages of the sites you visit cannot read it.

In the event of a data breach likely to put you at risk, you will be informed at your account's e-mail address, and the CNIL within 72 hours.

11. Minors

PanelFlow is not intended for people under 15, the age from which a minor can consent alone to the processing of their data in France. If you know of an account opened by a younger child, write to us: it will be deleted.

12. Changes

This policy is updated when the software changes what it records. The date at the top of the page is authoritative. It is versioned with the service's code, so every change is dated and explained.
