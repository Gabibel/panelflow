Keep PanelFlow in reach

Chrome hides every new extension behind the puzzle piece in the toolbar. Click it, then the pin next to PanelFlow.

PanelFlow

That button is where your library, your reading stats and the list of sites live. The reader itself also answers to Alt + R on any page.

When should the reader open?

PanelFlow watches the page you are on and works out whether it is a chapter. What it does next is up to you — and you can change it per site later, from the toolbar button.

A site that declares its own direction still wins over this, and changing the mode inside the reader is remembered.

One computer, or all of them

PanelFlow works completely without an account: your library, your progress and your saved chapters stay in this browser.

An account adds two things it cannot do alone — the same shelf on your other browsers and on the phone app, and a server that keeps checking your series for new chapters while Chrome is closed.
